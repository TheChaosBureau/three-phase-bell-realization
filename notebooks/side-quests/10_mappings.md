## Compressed summary

The deepest shared primitives across the languages are:

1. **A constrained linear state space**
2. **A quadratic overlap or energy geometry**
3. **A symmetry that selects natural modes**
4. **Projection rules that define observables**
5. **Local transitions plus global consistency constraints**
6. **Discrete labels emerging from continuous geometry**

And the recurring resulting structures are:

* orthogonality
* rotation/phase
* handed modes
* invariant decompositions
* sphere/projective pictures
* transport/flow formulations
* finite outcomes from continuous states

## One-sentence takeaway

What keeps reappearing is not “three-phase is quantum” or “coloring is power systems,” but a common backbone of **linearity + symmetry + overlap geometry + projection + closure**.
